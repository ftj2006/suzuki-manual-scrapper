Content:
Seat Heater System Description (If Equipped)
The seat heater system consists of a seat cushion heater incorporating a thermostat and seatback heater at each of driver and passenger seats, and a seat heater switch. The seat heater system is so designed that while the seat heater switch is “ON”, the seat temperature is maintained within the specified range repeating on and off of the thermostat, namely, when seat temperature concerned is below 30 °C (86 °F), the thermostat turns on the electric current and when the temperature goes up to 40 °C (104 °F), the thermostat turns off the current.

Content:
Rearend Door Assembly Removal and Installation
Rearend Door Assembly Components
The rearend door is heavy and the door falls rapidly from the open position when the door balancers are detached, and can cause personal injury.
Be very careful when opening and closing the rearend door after the door balancers are detached from the door.
Removal
Disconnect rearend door harness connector (1) and washer hose (5).
Remove rearend door balancers (2) from vehicle.
Remove rearend door hinge nuts (4), and then remove rearend door assembly (3) with rearend door hinges.
Remove rearend door hinges, rearend door balancers and/or rearend door opening weather-strip if necessary.
Improperly handling or disposing of the rearend door balancers (1) can result in personal injury.
Do not disassemble a balancer. The gas inside the balancer’s cylinder can cause personal injury.
Before discarding any rearend door balancer, a hole must be drilled in the cylinder part using a 2 to 3 mm (0.08 to 0.12 in.) drill (2) to release the inside gas.
Wear goggles when drilling the hole because metal chips produced by the drilling will be propelled by the gas discharged through the hole and may injure your eyes.
Failure to take proper precautions when handling the removed rearend door balancers (dampers) can cause damage to balancers or can cause them not to work properly after reinstallation.
Do not scratch the exposed surface of the piston rod. Also, do not allow paint or oil to touch the surface of the piston rod.
Do not turn the piston rod with the balancer fully extended.
Installation
Reverse removal procedure noting the following points.
Tighten bolts and nuts to specified torque.
Adjust door latch striker position.
Adjust door cushion to prevent door from contacting the body when closed.
Apply sealant “A” to contact face of door hinge (1) and apply grease “B” to rotating part of door hinge.

Sealant
99000–31110

Precautions for Body Structure
Operation
Failure to take proper precautions when performing body repair work can result in severe personal injury.
Take the following precautions to reduce the risk of injury.
Wear clothing and protective items suitable for operation.
Before attempting any body repair, disconnect negative (–) cable at battery.
When using body frame repair device for pulling-out operation, avoid standing in front of the pulling chain.
Whenever performing body panel work near fuel tank using flames or sparks, remove the fuel tank from the vehicle and prevent the removed fuel tank from fuel leakage referring to “Fuel Tank Removal and Installation” in Section 1G.
K14C model:
High-Strength Steel
Body panels of high-strength steel with the tensile strength more than 590 MPa (ultra-high-strength steel) will remarkably reduce its original strength if they are cut, heated and welded.
However, when repair is necessary, cut and connect the panel at the specified positions.
And, if the designated positions are not suitable for “cut and connect” due to the vehicle conditions, or the part is not required “cut and connect”, replace it with a new spare panel as supply unit.
Refer to
Sheet Metal Description
for tensile strength of steel panels.
Ambient temperature around auxiliary power module (lithium-ion battery) higher than 60 °C (140 °F) could spoil its original performance.
If estimated temperature exceeds 60 °C (140 °F) due to body repair work, remove the module beforehand.
Body Panel Repair
Failure to take proper precautions when performing body repair work can reduce original strength of the body.
The number of spot welded points should be increased by 20 to 30% in number from original welding points. The numbers and locations of the welding points in the drawings show the original spot welding.
Although the spot welding is recommended in the repair procedure, you shall adopt plug welding when the panel thickness for welding points exceeds the performance of your spot welding device. Take the following precautions to reduce the risk of the strength reduction.

Bench and jig systems are essential for accurate and efficient repairs of deformed body structures.

Contact the system manufacturer for details on installations and operations.

Removals of members, frames, struts and wheel housings are required before completing the frame deformation correction.
