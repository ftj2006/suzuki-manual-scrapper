Content:
Precautions for Service and Diagnosis of Seat Belt
If a seat belt is combined with any buckle that is not originally paired with the belt, the latch plate of the belt may not fully engage with the buckle and thus may not lock reliably when the belt is fastened. Always replace the seat belt and buckle as a set. SUZUKI only supplies the buckle and seat belt assembly packaged together as a set.
A seat belt pretensioner that has been activated can no longer protect the seat belt wearer.
Replace any seat belt whose pretensioner has been activated with a new seat belt and buckle set.
Before servicing or replacing seat belt assembly, refer to the following precautions.
Check if tongue plate and buckle are normal.
Keep sharp edges and damaging objects away from webbing.
Never bend or damage buckle and tongue plate.
Do not bleach or dye webbing. (Use only mild soap and lukewarm water to clean it.)
When installing a seat belt anchor bolt, first tighten it by hand to prevent cross-threading and then tighten to specified torque.
Do not attempt any repair on seat belt assembly. If any fault is found, replace the seat belt as an assembly.
Keep belts dry and clean at all times.
Any defective part must be replaced with a new one.
Replace seat belt assembly whose webbing is cut or damaged.
Do not put anything into trim panel opening which seat belt webbing passes through.
After an accident, whether the seat belt pretensioner has activated or not, perform checks and repairs described on
Repair and Inspection Required after Accident
.

Seat belt assembly with emergency locking retractor (ELR) is designed so that it locks immediately (to prevent the webbing from being pulled out of the retractor any further) when any of the following items is detected as exceeding each set value:
Speed at which the webbing is pulled out of the retractor

Seat Belt Assembly with ELR and Pretensioner
The seat belt assembly with ELR and a pretensioner has a pretensioner mechanism which operates in linkage with the air bag in addition to the described ELR.
The pretensioner is incorporated in retractor and controlled by SDM as one of air bag system components. It will be activated at the same time as the driver and passenger air bag (inflator) module when an impact at the front of vehicle exceeds the specified value.

Precautions for Air Bag System Service

Seat Belt Reminder
If driver, passenger or rear seat belt is unfastened under the following conditions, seat belt reminder light and warning buzzer inform that driver, passenger or rear seat belts is unfastened. Front seat belt reminder light and rear seat belt reminder lights are located in combination meter. Warning buzzer are located inside combination meter.
These reminder lights and warning buzzer operate as follows:
Driver and passenger seat belt reminders
Seat belt reminder light comes on if driver or passenger seat belt is unfastened when ignition is “ON”.
If vehicle runs with seat belt unfastened and the speed exceeds 15 km/h (9 mile/h), warning buzzer operates for approximately 95 seconds and the corresponding seat belt reminder light flashes synchronously with buzzer. When warning buzzer stops operating, the corresponding seat belt reminder light stays ON.
While vehicle is running at more than 15 km/h (9 mile/h) and if driver or passenger seat belt is unfastened, warning buzzer operates for approximately 95 seconds and the corresponding seat belt reminder light flashes synchronously with buzzer. When warning buzzer stops operating, the corresponding seat belt reminder light stays ON.

Failure to take proper precautions when servicing seat belt components or attempting to disassemble or repair the seat belt pretensioner may cause personal injury.
Never measure the resistance of a seat belt pretensioner with a tester. Electric current from the tester may trigger activation of the seat belt pretensioner.

If a seat belt pretensioner is dropped, it may not work normally.

Precautions for Air Bag System Service
Performing service operations on or around air bag system components or wiring without temporarily deactivating the air bag system may result in accidental activation of the air bag system. This could cause injury or the need for extra repairs to the air bag system.
Temporarily deactivate the air bag system before performing service operations on or around air bag system components.
Follow the procedures described in
Disabling Air Bag System
.
If the driver’s and front passenger’s seats are modified or accessories such as seat covers or cushions are attached to or put on the seats, the air bag system will not work properly, which increases the risk of personal injury.
Do not modify the front seats and avoid adding anything to them such as seat covers or cushions.
If you disconnect the connector from the air bag (inflator) module with a screwdriver or the like, static electricity may trigger deployment of the air bag, which could result in personal injury. Never insert a screwdriver or the like between the air bag (inflator) module and the connector.
When storing air bag system components, select a place where the ambient temperature is below 65 °C (149 °F).
When applying paint around the air bag system related parts, be careful that the harness or connector will not be exposed to the paint mist.

Passenger Air Bag Deactivation Switch Description (If Equipped)
The passenger air bag deactivation switch (1) is installed on the passenger side of the instrument panel. It is used to suspend the operation of passenger air bag (inflator) module for such cases as the child restraint is installed to the front passenger’s seat.
SDM determines whether to enable or disable operation of passenger air bag (inflator) module based on the air bag deactivation switch setting (Air bag ON/Air bag OFF). The deactivation switch setting can be changed in any ignition mode.
When ignition is set to “ON”, the SDM detects the setting position (Air bag ON/Air bag OFF) of the passenger air bag deactivation switch and determines whether to enable or disable operation of the passenger air bag (inflator) module. If deactivation switch is in Air bag OFF position, it turns on the “PASSENGER AIR BAG OFF” indicator (3) on between center ventilation louver of the instrument panel to indicate that passenger air bag (inflator) module is disabled.
In addition, if the passenger air bag deactivation switch setting is switched while ignition is “ON”, SDM does not store any DTC and does not turn on the “AIR BAG” warning light.
If the passenger air bag deactivation switch is in the “Air bag ON” position when the child restraint is in use, death or serious injury to the child may result due to deployment of the passenger air bag.
Set the passenger air bag deactivation switch to the “Air bag OFF” position when using the child restraint installed in the front passenger seat.
If the passenger air bag deactivation switch is in the “Air bag OFF” position when the child restraint is not in use, death or serious injury to the passenger may result due to non-activation of the passenger air bag.
Set the passenger air bag deactivation switch to the “Air bag ON” position when there is no child restraint installed on the front passenger seat.
2.
“PASSENGER AIR BAG ON” indicator
Enable Mode
When ignition is set to “ON”, both “PASSENGER AIR BAG ON” indicator (2) and “PASSENGER AIR BAG OFF” indicator (3) light up for a few seconds and then go out. However, if passenger air bag deactivation switch (1) is set to “ON” position (passenger air bag is activated: contact point closed), the passenger air bag activation indicator “PASSENGER AIR BAG ON” (“ON” lamp) lights up for about 1 minute and then goes out. In the enable mode, the air bag system is controlled in the usual manner.
Disable Mode
When ignition is set to “ON”, both “PASSENGER AIR BAG ON” indicator and “PASSENGER AIR BAG OFF” indicator light up for a few seconds and then go out. However, if passenger air bag deactivation switch is set to “OFF” position (passenger air bag is deactivated: contact point opened), the passenger air bag activation indicator “PASSENGER AIR BAG OFF” (“OFF” lamp) lights up and stays on. In the disable mode, the passenger air bag (inflator) module (5) is always disabled.
Fail-Safe Operation
When a trouble occurs in the passenger air bag deactivation switch and SDM (4) cannot judge the trouble condition of the switch, SDM turns on “AIR BAG” warning light to show occurrence of an abnormal condition. The operation mode of the passenger air bag (inflator) module depends on the condition when the trouble occurred.

Self-Diagnosis Function
SDM also has self-diagnosis function that memorizes DTC when an error is detected. After understanding the following descriptions, perform troubleshooting referring to
Air Bag Diagnostic System Check
when an error is detected.
SDM always monitors system while ignition is “ON”. If an error is detected, it notifies driver of error by turning on “AIR BAG” warning light (1) and detected error is stored in SDM memory as DTC.
“AIR BAG” warning light turns on for about 6 seconds with ignition “ON” to indicate that it operates properly and turns off if no error is found in air bag system.
