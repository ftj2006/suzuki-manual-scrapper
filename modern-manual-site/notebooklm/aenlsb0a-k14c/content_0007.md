Cylinder Head Inspection
Valve and Cylinder Head Disassembly and Reassembly:K14C
Remove all carbon deposits from combustion chambers.
Metal surfaces can be scuffed or nicked when removing carbon deposits with sharp-edged tool.
Do not use any sharp-edged tool to scrape off carbon deposits from engine parts.
Check cylinder head for crack on intake and exhaust ports, combustion chambers and head surface.
Cylinder Head Flatness
Using straightedge (1) and feeler gauge (2), check flatness of gasket surface at a total of 6 positions.
If any measured value exceeds its limit, correct gasket surface with a surface plate and abrasive paper #400 (waterproof silicon carbide abrasive paper) by placing abrasive paper on surface plate and rubbing gasket surface against paper to grind high spots.
If it is impossible to correct cylinder head distortion, replace cylinder head.
Distorted mating surface of cylinder head and cylinder head gasket causes combustion gas and/or coolant leakage, overheating and reduced power output.
Distortion of mating surface of cylinder head and cylinder head gasket
Limit: 0.03 mm (0.0012 in.)

Using straightedge (1) and feeler gauge (2), check flatness of manifold seating surface.
If measured value exceeds its limit, correct seating surface or replace cylinder head.

## AENLSB0A1421001 - Engine Construction Description
Path: ./source-mirror/SWIFT_A2L310_A2L412_A2L414/xml/AENLSB0A/AENLSB0A1421001.xml
Tree scopes: unscoped
Outgoing references: none

Engine Construction Description
The engine is a water-cooled, in-line 4-cylinder, 4-stroke-cycle direct-injection gasoline unit with a DOHC valve mechanism arranged in a “V” type valve configuration and has 16 valves.
Intake camshaft (1) and exhaust camshaft (2) are mounted over the cylinder head and are driven by the crankshaft with a timing chain. The intake camshaft is equipped with a VVT actuator (3) controlled by ECM, a high pressure fuel pump cam (4) and a signal rotor at the end. The exhaust camshaft is equipped with a conventional hydraulic VVT actuator (5) controlled by OCV on the oil pump assembly.
The valves are operated by roller type rocker arms (6) driven by intake and exhaust camshafts. And, end of the valve rocker arms are supported by hydraulic valve lash adjusters (7) which require no valve clearance adjustment.
Exhaust manifold is incorporated in the cylinder head and cooled by coolant.
The engine is equipped with piston cooling jets (8) and the injection is controlled by piston cooling jet valve (9) for effective cooling of pistons, oil cooler, intercooler and turbocharger (10), and the turbocharger is mounted on the cylinder head directly. Further, a high pressure fuel pump (11) and a vacuum pump (12) are mounted on the cylinder head at the rear side and driven by the intake camshaft.
An oil pressure and temperature sensor (13) is located on the timing chain cover and monitors temperature and pressure of the engine oil.
This engine has adopted cracked connecting rods (14). The connecting rod formed as a single piece is cracked apart at the cracking line and then combined in one using connecting rod bolts without using dowel pins. This method ensures a stable fitting between connecting rod and its cap reducing the weight.
With this direct-injection system, intake air is supercharged into the combustion chamber (15) by the turbocharger and the air is highly compressed in the compression stroke and, before the end of the compression stroke, fuel is injected through fuel injector (16) at high pressure into the combustion chamber directly and ignited by long reach spark plug. This fuel combustion generates more engine power with low emission.
Crankcase ventilation is managed using two routes. A breather plate (17) adjoining the cylinder block provides labyrinth and separates oil and blow-by gas, and the blow-by gas is sucked in intake manifold through PCV valve (18) and the separated oil returns in oil pan. As another ventilation route, a breather hose is provided between the upper part of the cylinder head cover and air cleaner outlet hose.

## AENLSB0A1421002 - VVT System Description

Tree scopes: unscoped
Outgoing references: none

VVT System Description
The VVT system for this engine controls the intake camshaft with a electric motor drive VVT actuator and exhaust camshaft with a conventional hydraulic VVT actuator.
The dual VVT system improves the engine performance by continuously varying both intake and exhaust valve timing most suitably according to the engine operating condition. The electric motor drive intake VVT has a characteristic to shift the phase much wider regardless of the engine oil temperature and engine speed. And, the combined effect of the two VVTs has attained engine power, fuel efficiency and low emission all together properly.
System Description
Rotational position of the intake sprocket, exhaust sprocket and crankshaft timing sprocket is fixed each other engaged with a timing chain.
However, the camshaft timing is varied when ECM controls the electric motor for intake VVT actuator and OCV for exhaust VVT actuator to advance or retard each camshaft. Inside of each VVT actuator has independent device which can rotate separately from the actuator housing (chain engaged sprocket side) and is directly fixed to the camshaft by a bolt. Therefore, the phase between the VVT actuator (sprocket side) and the camshaft can be changed accordingly.
This VVT system can vary the valve timing within a range of 70° in crankshaft rotation angle for intake camshaft and 50° for exhaust camshaft.
Operation Description
Intake VVT actuator (Electric motor system)
For intake VVT, the electric motor part (1) of the intake VVT is mounted on the timing chain cover and, the motor rotation is decelerated by the inside reduction mechanism to synchronize with the cam speed and outputted to a coupling (2). Sprocket side VVT actuator (drive system) (3) includes the intake sprocket and the central part (separated device) in the actuator is secured on the camshaft (4) by a bolt (5). The central part is driven by the motor coupling through an eccentric shaft (6) and changes the phase from the sprocket. When the motor coupling rotates higher than the sprocket (cam phase goes ahead of sprocket), the cam timing is advanced and when it is lower than the sprocket (cam phase retards from sprocket), the cam timing is retarded.
Exhaust VVT actuator (Hydraulic system)
For exhaust VVT, OCV (7) switches and adjusts the oil pressure applied on VVT actuator (8) by moving the valve spool in OCV according to the duty cycle pulses from ECM.
VVT actuator receives oil pressure from OCV at chambers for timing advancing (9) or retarding (10) which are separated by the rotor (11).
The rotor turns according to change in balance between the hydraulic pressures applied to both chambers. Sprocket and actuator housing are combined in one, and the rotor is secured on the camshaft (12) by a bolt (13). Therefore, the rotation of the rotor (camshaft) changes the phase from the sprocket.

Turbocharger Description
Turbocharger System
The turbocharger system consists of a turbocharger, intercooler which cools pressurized air for increasing the volume density and charging efficiency, waste gate valve to control exhaust gas flow for optimum supercharge and ABV.
Turbocharger
The turbine / compressor shaft is supported with a semi-floating metal lubricated by pressurized engine oil from the main oil gallery through an oil union bolt providing mesh filter and responds to extremely high revolution.
Waste Gate Valve Function
The waste gate valve (1) incorporated in the turbocharger is controlled by ECM with waste gate valve-vacuum switching valve (WGV-VSV) using negative pressure from the vacuum pump. The valve is open as an initial state and at the time of the engine start, exhaust gas passes both turbocharger turbine and waste gate. And, when negative pressure is transmitted from the vacuum pump to the waste gate valve actuator (2), the waste gate valve actuator rod (3) pulls to close the valve if necessary in response to a demand for the supercharge i.e. engine power.
Air Bypass Valve Function
ABV (4) incorporated in the turbocharger is electrically controlled by ECM and the solenoid valve opens to release compressed air from turbocharger outlet side (3) to inlet side (2) when throttle valve (7) is closed suddenly while the vehicle is running with wide open throttle. The open function of the ABV releases the air pressure for an instant to restrain air surge noise.
