Precautions for Adaptive Cruise Control System
Adaptive cruise control system will not supplement lack of driving skills or reckless driving.
While driving with this system, do not overestimate the system’s abilities and pay enough attention to ambient traffic and road conditions, weather, etc.
Failure to take the following precautions may cause unexpected accident.
When a vehicle ahead is getting extremely closer or a vehicle cuts in, maintain safe distance between two vehicles without having too much confidence in the adaptive cruise control system, operating brakes and avoid rear-end collision.
If the vehicle ahead disappears while the adaptive cruise control system is functioning, your vehicle will be accelerated automatically up to the set speed. Therefore, it is necessary to cancel the control in the system before leaving main lane of the highway.
The adaptive cruise control system does not respond to a stationary vehicle. Therefore, secure safety against the pressing hazard paying enough attention to road conditions and operating brakes.
If the road shape repeats steep uphill and downhill, the system may fail to pick up a running vehicle ahead and it comes closer. In this situation, it is necessary to apply brakes.
Failure to observe the following precautions will cause malfunction of adaptive cruise control system resulting in a serious accident.
Do not affix sticker including clear film to front surface of the RBS control module.
Do not drop or otherwise expose the RBS control module to large shock. The RBS control module that has received large shock should be replaced.
If peripheral component of the radiator upper grill is damaged, check the RBS control module and its bracket.
When replacing the front bumper, use SUZUKI genuine parts.
Do not modify the suspension. Modification of the vehicle’s height or inclination may affect to pick up a running vehicle ahead correctly.
The adaptive cruise control system functions detecting reflected radio wave from rear end of the vehicle ahead, therefore the system may not work under the following conditions.
A vehicle ahead or a vehicle on the neighboring lane is running splashing rainwater or snow dust.
A vehicle ahead has extremely small rear end area such as an unloaded trailer.
Your vehicle is tilted caused by heavy loading on the luggage compartment or rear seat.
Even when distance is short between two vehicles, headway alert may not work under the following conditions.
Your vehicle is running at 160 km/h (99 mile/h) or higher speed.
Relative speed to a vehicle ahead is small, i.e. two vehicles are running almost at the same speed.
A vehicle ahead is running faster, i.e. the vehicle ahead is going farther gradually.
Right after setting up the desired speed.
While depressing the accelerator pedal or right after releasing the pedal.
When the millimeter wave radar is heavily covered by raindrops or snow, the system may not detect the correct distance to the vehicle ahead.
The millimeter wave radar detects soil on the radar surface and informs this situation to the driver.
When dirty (or reduced visibility) condition is detected, adaptive cruise control system is canceled automatically.
However, it may not be detected even if the surface is soiled depending on the conditions.
Therefore, it is necessary to keep millimeter wave radar always clean.
Extent of detecting area by the millimeter wave radar is narrow at nearby area in front of your vehicle and distance to the vehicle ahead cannot be maintained properly due to delay in detecting motorcycle running on the edge of your lane or vehicle closely cutting in.
Because of the road conditions such as winding road, getting in or getting out of a corner, inside of tunnel, narrow traffic lane due to road repairing or traffic restriction, or the vehicle conditions such as steering operation, vehicle position in the lane, unstable vehicle running due to vehicle damage caused by accident, your vehicle is controlled temporarily detecting a vehicle on neighboring lane or peripheral things, or warning is activated. In this situation, affected by the poor detecting condition, your vehicle may come near the vehicle ahead accidentally.

Adaptive Cruise Control System Construction
In addition to a constant-speed control like a conventional cruise control system, the adaptive cruise control system has a constant headway gap control which keeps distance to a vehicle ahead.
Adaptive cruise control system consists of RBS control module, cruise control switch, ECM, ESP ® control module, lighting and turn signal switch, combination meter, TCM (A/T model), dual sensor brake support control module and BCM.
The adaptive cruise control system adopts a millimeter (radio) wave radar system as a sensor to detect distance to the vehicle ahead and relative speed.
Distance switch is incorporated in the cruise control switch and located in the steering wheel right side. Using this switch, vehicle distance can be set to any one of “Long”, “Middle” and “Short” modes.
For driver’s visual recognition, the state of the control by the adaptive cruise control system is shown on the information display in the combination meter.

Component
Description
RBS control module
RBS control module incorporates a radar sensor and is located behind the radiator upper grill. This control module determines the vehicle to follow and target level of acceleration / deceleration based on information obtained by millimeter wave radar, and demands brake control and torque control for adequate distance to the vehicle ahead.
The radar sensor adopts a millimeter wave radar method and transmits a radio wave from the front of the RBS control module, and measures reflex time of the radio wave from the vehicle ahead. Thus the radar sensor measures the distance, relative speed and direction of the vehicle ahead. Since the radar sensor uses a radio wave, effective detection of the vehicle ahead is possible under the condition of high speed running, at night, in bad weather such as rainy weather or dense fog.
RBS control module communicates with other control module through CAN communication. For CAN communication, refer to “Communication System Description” in Section 10H.
K14C model:
CRUISE (main) switch
This switch has a momentary contact type button to turn adaptive cruise control function ON and OFF.
The switch signal is outputted to ECM and RBS control module receives cruise control switch signal from ECM through CAN communication.
Distance switch
Vehicle distance under “Constant headway gap control” can be changed to “Long”, “Middle” or “Short” mode using this switch.
The switch signal is outputted to ECM and RBS control module receives cruise control switch signal from ECM through CAN communication.
SET/– switch
When this switch is pressed (ON) and then released (OFF) while the vehicle is running at 40 km/h (25 mile/h) or higher speed, the vehicle speed obtained when the switch is released is stored in memory and it is set as a target vehicle speed under adaptive cruise control.
When this switch is pressed (ON) for a momentarily during adaptive cruise control, the set speed will decrease by 1 km/h (1 mile/h). When this switch is continuously pressed (ON) during adaptive cruise control, the set speed will decrease by 5 km/h (5 mile/h) as long as the switch is ON. And the vehicle is controlled according to the new set speed.
The switch signal is outputted to ECM and RBS control module receives cruise control switch signal from ECM through CAN communication.
RES/+ switch
When this switch is pressed (ON) for a momentarily during adaptive cruise control, the set speed will increase by 1 km/h (1 mile/h). When this switch is continuously pressed (ON) during adaptive cruise control, the set speed will increase by 5 km/h (5 mile/h) as long as the switch is ON. And the vehicle is controlled according to the new set speed.
If vehicle speed is higher than 40 km/h (25 mile/h) after adaptive cruise control is canceled, pressing this switch ON momentarily will resume the vehicle running at the speed.
The switch signal is outputted to ECM and RBS control module receives cruise control switch signal from ECM through CAN communication.
CANCEL switch
When this switch is pressed (ON), adaptive cruise control is canceled.
The switch signal is outputted to ECM and RBS control module receives cruise control switch signal from ECM through CAN communication.
ECM
ECM controls electric throttle valve opening based on information from RBS control module through CAN communication.
ECM transmits cruise control switch signal, engine torque signal, vehicle speed signal, etc. to RBS control module through CAN communication.
ESP ® control module
ESP ® control module controls brake pressure and brake light based on information from RBS control module through CAN communication.
ESP ® control module transmits wheel speed signal, lateral G signal, etc. to RBS control module through CAN communication.
Lighting and turn signal switch
Steering angle sensor is incorporated, and transmits steering angle signal to RBS control module through CAN communication.
Neutral position switch (M/T model)
This switch is installed on the transaxle case. The ECM determines whether the gear is in neutral based on the signal from this switch.
Combination meter
Combination meter turns on or off adaptive cruise control warning light, shows state of the control on information display and sounds warning buzzer based on information through CAN communication.
TCM (A/T model)
TCM transmits transmission shift position signal to RBS control module through CAN communication.
BCM
BCM transmits turn signal switch status etc. to RBS control module through CAN communication.
RBS control module uses turn signal switch status to judge overtaking action.
Dual sensor brake support control module
Dual sensor brake support control module transmits dual sensor brake support control request signal etc. to RBS control module through CAN communication.

Description of Adaptive Cruise Control System Function
Adaptive cruise control system controls vehicle speed and vehicle distance as follows.
State of control
Basic control (as a set speed 100 km/h (62 mile/h))
Constant-speed control [A]
Without detecting vehicle ahead
Vehicle runs at the set speed without driver’s operation of accelerator pedal.
Deceleration control [B]
When detecting a vehicle ahead running at lower speed than your vehicle during constant-speed control
Corresponding to speed of the vehicle running ahead, deceleration control is activated using throttle valve control and brake control. Brake light is turned on during this brake control.
When the distance is getting too close due to sudden deceleration of the vehicle ahead or cutting-in of another vehicle, headway alert (sound of warning buzzer and indication of information display) will operate.
Constant headway gap control [C]
When following the vehicle ahead running slower than your vehicle
Your vehicle runs corresponding to speed of the vehicle running ahead under “Constant headway gap control” keeping the set distance according to the vehicle speed.
Acceleration control [D]
When the vehicle ahead running at lower speed than your vehicle disappears from detecting area
At the moment the vehicle ahead disappears, your vehicle accelerates slowly up to the set speed and runs at a constant speed.
Effective Conditions for Control
If all of the following conditions are met, adaptive cruise control system operates.
CRUISE (main) switch is turned ON.
Vehicle is running at 40 km/h (25 mile/h) or higher speed.
Gear shift lever is in 3rd or higher position. (M/T model)
Select lever is “D” or “M” range. (A/T model)
Brake pedal is released.
Parking brake is released.
Clutch pedal is released. (M/T model)
ESP ® OFF mode is not selected.
ESP ® is not operating.
DSBS is not operating.
Driver side door is closed.
Driver seat belt is fastened.
Engine speed is within specified range.
Adaptive cruise control system is in normal condition.
Speed Set Function
When SET/– switch is pressed in execution state of the control, the speed obtained when the switch is pressed is stored as a set speed and the adaptive cruise control system starts control.
Set speed becomes a target speed in “Constant-speed control”, furthermore it becomes the maximum speed in “Constant headway gap control”.
Acceleration Function
When RES/+ switch is pressed for a momentarily, the vehicle speed is set to 1 km/h (1 mile/h) high and the switch is pressed for a long time, the vehicle speed is set to 5 km/h (5 mile/h) high under the control of the adaptive cruise control system and the vehicle is accelerated according to the new set speed.
During the “Constant headway gap control”, the vehicle is not accelerated but set speed can be changed.
Deceleration Function
When SET/– switch is pressed for a momentarily, the vehicle speed is set to 1 km/h (1 mile/h) low and the switch is pressed for a long time, the vehicle speed is set to 5 km/h (5 mile/h) low under the control of the adaptive cruise control system and the vehicle is decelerated according to the new set speed.
Deceleration function by SET/– switch is performed by throttle valve control but brake control is not applied.
Cancel Function
When CANCEL switch is pressed under the control of the adaptive cruise control system, the control is canceled and the system becomes in a state of on standby.
Resume Function
When the vehicle control is on standby by cancel operation while the vehicle is running at 40 km/h (25 mile/h) or higher speed, pressing RES/+ switch allows the system to resume running the vehicle at the speed set before the control is canceled.
Headway Gap Setting Function
There are three modes of “Long”, “Middle” and “Short” in vehicle distance setting and, during the control of the adaptive cruise control system or in the state of standby, the mode can be changed by pressing the distance switch. RBS control module controls the vehicle in “Constant headway gap control” calculating the headway gap based on the vehicle distance setting and your vehicle speed.
When ignition is set “ON”, the headway gap is set in “Middle” mode.

Headway Alert Function
When the headway gap is getting too close due to sudden deceleration of the vehicle ahead or cutting-in of another vehicle and pressing collision hazard is judged, sounding warning buzzer and indication of information display warn the driver of the situation.

## AENLSB0AA121005 - Speed Limiter System Construction

Tree scopes: bookmarks: 10 - Control Systems > 10A - Cruise Control System > General Description > Adaptive Cruise Control
Outgoing references: none

Content:
Speed Limiter System Construction
Speed limiter system is a system to control the vehicle speed not to exceed maximum speed which is set by the driver within the vehicle speed of 30 to 240 km/h (20 to 150 mile/h).
The system mainly consists of electric throttle body assembly, APP sensor, ECM, cruise control switches (LIMIT switch, SET/– switch, RES/+ switch and CANCEL switch), etc.
Speed Limiter Function
While speed limiter system is functioning, the maximum speed is indicated in the information display and, when accelerator pedal is depressed, the system controls vehicle speed not to exceed the preset maximum speed by electric throttle valve opening.
Warning Function
If vehicle speed exceeds the preset maximum speed at downhill driving etc., the maximum speed indication in the information display blinks and warning buzzer sounds intermittently.
Temporary Canceling Function
If accelerator pedal is depressed quickly and deeply, the system intermits the speed control and vehicle speed changes depending on accelerator pedal operation even above the preset maximum speed, and the maximum speed indication in the information display blinks and warning buzzer sounds once.
The system keeps the intermittent speed control until the vehicle speed becomes lower than the preset maximum speed.

Content:
Adaptive Cruise Control System Self-Diagnosis Function
Description
RBS control module and ECM monitor conditions of adaptive cruise control system components and circuits. When an abnormal condition in the system occurs, RBS control module and ECM operate as follows.
Self-Diagnosis Function
RBS control module and ECM have self-diagnosis function that stores DTC for abnormalities detected in adaptive cruise control system. Stored DTCs remain in memory of RBS control module and ECM unless they are cleared.
RBS control module does not store history DTCs of C1610, C1640, C1655, C1658 and C1659.
Warning Function
RBS control module turns on the adaptive cruise control warning light (1) to warn the driver that a malfunction is detected.
Fail-Safe Control
When any abnormality is found, RBS control module disables adaptive cruise control.
