Valve Body Assembly Removal and Installation
The transaxle side oil pan may become deformed during removal. If the removed transaxle side oil pan is reused, it may cause oil leakage. Do not reuse the side oil pan. Always install a new one.
The transaxle side oil pan bolts are pre-coated with adhesive. If the bolts are reused, it may work loose.
Replace the transaxle side oil pan bolts with new ones.
When valve body assembly is removed or replaced, perform
TCM Learning Value Clearance
,
Neutral Position Calibration
and
TCM Learning Value Initialization
.
Removal
Disconnect negative (–) cable at battery.
Hoist vehicle.
Remove engine undercover.
Remove left side engine under side cover.
Drain A/T fluid.
Drain coolant.
Remove radiator outlet hose.
Remove ATF cooler hose No.1.
Remove transaxle side oil pan (1).
Remove ATF temperature sensor clamp bolt, and then remove ATF temperature sensor (1) and ATF temperature sensor clamp (2).
Disconnect solenoid connectors (1) from solenoid valves.
Disconnect output shaft speed sensor connector (1) from harness clamp.
Remove valve body bolts, and then remove valve body assembly from transaxle case, being careful not to drop manual valve.
There are three types of bolts (bolts A (1), B (2) and C (3)) for fixing valve body assembly.

Solenoid Valve Inspection
ATF Temperature Sensor Inspection

TCM Removal and Installation
As the TCM consists of precision parts, the TCM will be damaged if it is exposed to an excessively large impact.
Handle the TCM carefully so as not to impact it.
When TCM is removed or replaced, perform
TCM Learning Value Clearance
,
Neutral Position Calibration
and
TCM Learning Value Initialization
.
Removal
With select lever in P range, apply parking brake and block wheels.
Remove battery and battery tray.
Remove select cable clip and disconnect select cable from manual select lever.
Move manual select lever (1) to “N” position (2), and then remove manual select lever.
If torque is applied to the manual shaft when loosening the manual select lever nut, internal part of the A/T may be damaged.
Fix the manual select lever in place before loosening the manual select lever nut.
Disconnect connector (1) from TCM (2).
Remove TCM bolts, and then pull TCM straight up to remove it.
Installation
Install solenoid harness connector (1) to transaxle case correctly.
Install TCM by pushing it straight into transaxle case, and then tighten TCM bolts (1) to specified torque.

Manual Transaxle Construction
The transaxle provides six forward speeds and one reverse speed with four synchromesh devices and three shafts: input shaft, countershaft and reverse gear shaft.
The low speed sleeve & hub is mounted on countershaft and engaged with countershaft 1st gear or 2nd gear, while the high speed sleeve & hub is mounted on input shaft and engaged with input shaft 3rd gear or 4th gear. The 5th & 6th speed sleeve & hub is mounted on input shaft and engaged with input shaft 5th gear or 6th gear. The reverse gear sleeve & hub is mounted on reverse gear shaft and engaged with reverse idler left gear or reverse idler right gear.
The triple cone synchronizing mechanism is provided to 1st and 2nd gear synchromesh devices for high performance of shifting to 2nd gear in particular. The double cone synchronizing mechanism is provided to 3rd gear synchromesh device for high performance of shifting to 3rd gear.
The gear shift control lever provides a pull-up-for-reverse mechanism. Pulling up the reverse inhibit ring under the gear shift control lever knob releases the selection restriction and allows shifting of the gear to reverse.
For servicing, it is necessary to use genuine sealant or its equivalent on mating surfaces of transaxle case. It is also important that all parts are thoroughly cleaned with cleaning fluid and air-dried before reassembling.
Further, care must be taken to adjust between case and shaft to thrust clearance of input shaft, countershaft, reverse gear shaft and differential assembly. Synchronizer rings are inhibited from being lapped with respective gear cones using lapping compound before they are assembled.
